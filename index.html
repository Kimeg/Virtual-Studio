Pick a genre to load an example, then edit it. All tracks shows every instrument on one sheet; Track editor zooms into one. Choose straight or triplet note lengths, then click or drag on the grid to place notes; click a note to remove it. Triplet lengths affect note duration. Click a row label to hear that sound.
